    Robot lab

    Robot

    A robot is its DSIM build plus a range for everything not measured yet. Training draws a robot from those ranges every match: the narrower and truer they are, the truer everything it learns.

    Import from DSIM

    1. In DSIM (localhost:5173) build the robot, then open the console (Safari: Develop → Show JavaScript Console).
    2. Paste copy(localStorage['decodesim.settings.v1']) and press Return: DSIM's robot is now on your clipboard.
    3. Paste it below. A replay file works too.

    Shooting zone

    Where the robot may shoot from. DSIM's 3D solve decides where a shot settles; the zone narrows that to where you trust the real robot. The robot only drives to scoring spots inside it and only holds fire inside it. Draw an area: click the field to add a corner, drag a corner to move it, double-click a corner to remove it.

    Start from

    Saved with the robot. Playbook and team-play entries made under another zone show as outdated and are planned again on the next build.
