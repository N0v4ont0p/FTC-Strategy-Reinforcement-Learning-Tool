Training data

Your DSIM replays in Training data/. Each one is re-simulated in DSIM and every decision in it (which group, FLOWER, shoot, human player) becomes a lesson. Generation 0 starts from a network fitted to them, and every generation a share of robots are students: a parent that takes a short lesson from these replays and from the champion's own decisions. How big that share is follows how well students do.

Refresh re-simulates the ticked replays and refits (a few seconds per replay); the open run then learns from the new set from its next generation. In DSIM, save a replay with Records → the run → ↓ Data.

How generation — was made

elite = kept unchanged · champion = the validated best, always kept · mutant = small nudges to some genes · crossover = genes mixed from two parents · behaviour mutation = a large change proven to alter what it chooses (or a skill setting) · student = a parent after a lesson from your replays · random = a brand-new robot. "Behaviour" is the share of test decisions where it now chooses differently from its parent.
