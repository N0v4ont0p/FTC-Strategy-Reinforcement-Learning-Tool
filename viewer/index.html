    Team plays

    The alliance plays together: a play gives each robot a role for AUTO, TELEOP and the last 30 s — FLOWERs · ground, each robot one side of the HIVE, both on the target side, tip cycle · support, joint volleys, who parks early… The search scores every play beside each kind of partner in DSIM on shared luck, then mutates and crosses the best (a zone line moves, the robots swap, a preference grows, a phase takes another's role) to discover plays nobody drew up. Finalists are re-scored on fresh luck. Training plays inside the winners.

    By champion

    mistakes per exam match

    Every champion's exam matches are audited: empty trips (a collecting job that got nothing), blocked shots, idle spells (3 s or more standing still, not parking), fouls by rule, stalls (20 s without progress), crashes into the HIVE frame, and judgement mistakes — decisions where thinking ahead beat the network by more than 5 points (from its thinking-ahead exam). A repeat is one the previous champion made too: same match, same moment and place. Each mistake becomes a drill: every 4th training match starts 3 s before one, exactly as it happened, and the champion thinks it through.

    The race

    Each generation the champion and every contender play the same brand-new matches with the same luck. A contender takes over when its lead passes the boundary (z ≥ 2.3: safe although it is looked at every generation) and it is not worse in its bad matches (its worst fifth). Clearly worse ones, and ones that could not prove themselves in 96 matches, are dropped.

    Gap report

    Measured the same way for everyone, from DSIM state.     Training data

    Your DSIM replays in Training data/. Each one is re-simulated in DSIM and every decision in it (which group, FLOWER, shoot, human player) becomes a demonstration. A network fitted to them races the champion from generation 0, and your decisions are mixed into what every candidate learns, fading out as the robot's own what-if lessons take over (the run's replay weight and fade). They are also the "you" row of the gap report.

    Refresh re-simulates the ticked replays and refits (a few seconds per replay); the open run then learns from the new set from its next generation. In DSIM, save a replay with Records → the run → ↓ Data.

    Evaluate a policy

    Full matches on held-out seeds (never used for lessons, the race or the exam); the reward is DSIM's score minus fouls DSIM does not call. Runs between generations.
